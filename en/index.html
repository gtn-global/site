01 — WHO WE ARE

Gateway To New

 

Gateway To New (GTN) is a cross-border business and industry partnership platform that connects international business communities with Chinese companies, industry expertise, and business networks.

We work with trade organizations, business associations, and industry leaders to facilitate meaningful connections and explore practical opportunities for collaboration between China and the world.

Our work focuses on three areas: industry insights and business intelligence, connections with relevant companies and industry experts, and collaborative programs such as business briefings, roundtables, and industry visits. Through these activities, we help our partners better understand relevant industries, identify potential partners, and develop opportunities for cross-border engagement.

GTN provides research, connections, and platforms for business dialogue. Commercial outcomes depend on the nature and circumstances of each project, and we do not guarantee orders or transactions.

11 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Get these clear before you go global.

What does GTN do?

We give Chinese management a going-global decision diagnosis: laying out the options, risks and costs of whether, where and how to enter — and offering a judgment you can discuss.

Can you guarantee deals or orders?

No. We provide judgment and connections, not promises of orders or deals.

How are events priced?

See the schedule page and each event page for specifics.

Dong Lixin also serves at WTC Singapore — what's his relation to GTN?

Dong Lixin also serves as Secretary-General & COO of WTC Singapore and Secretary-General of WTC One Club. GTN's paid services are provided by GTN; where events involve partner institutions, we clearly state each party's role.

What is the Global CEO China Tour?

We invite foreign entrepreneurs to China for industry visits — one sector and one source country/region per edition. Chinese companies may apply to be host companies and are confirmed after screening.
