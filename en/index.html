07 — FAQ

FAQ

What does GTN do?

We run decision diagnostics for the leadership of Chinese companies: whether to go abroad, where, and how to enter, laying out the options, risks and costs so you get a judgment you can debate.

Can you guarantee deals or orders?

No. We provide judgment and connections to resources, not guaranteed orders or deals.

How are events priced?

See the calendar and each event page for details.

How does the founder's role at WTC Singapore relate to GTN?

Dong Lixin is also Secretary-General and COO of WTC Singapore and Secretary-General of WTC One Club. GTN's paid services are provided by GTN; where an event involves those institutions, we state each party's role clearly.

What is the CEO tour?

We invite foreign business leaders to China for industry visits, one industry and one source country or region per edition. Chinese companies can apply to be host companies; hosts are confirmed after screening.

ABOUT

About GTN

Gateway To New (GTN) was founded by Dong Lixin (董立鑫). We give leadership teams of SRDI little giants, tech companies and listed firms a decision diagnostic for going global: judge first, then act. We connect global industry experts, real events and field intelligence, and lay out the options, risks and costs of whether, where and how to enter.

Founder Dong Lixin: Partner at HeJun Consulting; Secretary-General and COO of WTC Singapore; Secretary-General of WTC One Club.
