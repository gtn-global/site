Bruce Dong

Founder of Global Talent Network (GTN)　·　Globalization Strategy Advisor | Connecting Talents, Industries and Global Opportunities

Bruce Dong (Dong Lixin) is the Founder of Global Talent Network (GTN) and Business Director of WTC Singapore, a platform focused on connecting global talents, industries, and future opportunities. He also serves as Secretary General & COO of WTC Singapore and Partner at Hejun Consulting. With 20 years of experience across German manufacturing enterprises, China's digital economy, and international business networks, Bruce Dong focuses on China companies going global, globalization strategy, AI-driven business transformation, and cross-border ecosystem development.

About Bruce DongABOUT

Globalization is not simply about entering overseas markets. It is about creating connections: between talents and opportunities, between industries and global resources, between companies and future growth.

Bruce Dong is the Founder of Global Talent Network (GTN). Founded in 2024, GTN is an international growth platform dedicated to connecting global talents, industries, and future opportunities. Through initiatives including Go Beyond Workshop, Go Beyond Program, and Go Beyond Lab, GTN explores new ways to connect talent development, industry transformation, and global collaboration.

Why GTN Was CreatedWHY GTN

Throughout his career, Bruce Dong has worked across different business environments: German manufacturing; China's digital economy; AI-driven industry transformation; international business networks.

These experiences shaped his belief: Global expansion requires more than market opportunities. Companies need strategic understanding, industry insight, international connections, and the ability to build sustainable capabilities overseas.

GTN was created to become a bridge connecting global talents, business opportunities, industrial resources, and future possibilities.

Globalization PerspectiveGLOBAL PERSPECTIVE

Bruce Dong's understanding of globalization has been shaped by experiences across manufacturing, technology, and international business ecosystems.

German Manufacturing Experience · Roto Frank
From 2008 to 2021, Bruce Dong was involved in the development of Roto Frank's China operations and eventually served as Branch General Manager. This experience provided deep insights into German manufacturing excellence, industrial operations, long-term business development, and cross-cultural management.

Digital Business Experience · JD.com
At JD.com, one of China's leading technology-driven commerce companies, Bruce Dong gained experience in digital business models, platform operations, supply chain efficiency, and technology-enabled growth.

Industry Digitalization Experience · BEKE (KE Holdings)
At BEKE, Bruce Dong participated in industry digitalization initiatives, focusing on AI application, data-driven operations, and digital transformation of traditional industries.
